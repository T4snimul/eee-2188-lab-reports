\section{Result and Discussion}
\markright{Result and Discussion}

The experimental result and discussion show that the diode clipper and clamper circuits behave consistently with theory: the clipper circuits remove the selected positive or negative half-cycle, while the clamper circuits shift the waveform baseline without eliminating an entire half-cycle. The observed output waveforms also reflect practical diode voltage drop and component tolerance, but the overall waveform shapes and bias-induced shifts matched the expected behavior for both biased and unbiased configurations.
