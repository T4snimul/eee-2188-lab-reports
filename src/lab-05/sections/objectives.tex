\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item To study the operation of positive and negative diode clipper circuits.
  \item To study the operation of positive and negative diode clamper circuits.
  \item To observe how DC bias and a coupling capacitor change the output waveform shape and level.
  \item To compare experimental results with theory for waveform clipping and level shifting.
\end{enumerate}
