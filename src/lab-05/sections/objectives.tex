\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item To study the operation of positive and negative diode clipper circuits.
  \item To observe how the clipping level changes when a DC bias is added to the diode circuit.
  \item To compare the output waveforms obtained for different input signal types such as sine, square, and triangular waves.
  \item To verify the theoretical behavior of clipper circuits through experimental observation and analysis.
\end{enumerate}
