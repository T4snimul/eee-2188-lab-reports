\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

This experiment successfully demonstrated the operation of diode clipper circuits and confirmed that clipping depends on the polarity of the diode and the presence of bias voltage. The observed outputs validated the theoretical concept that clippers can limit either the positive or negative portion of a waveform and shift the clipping level when a bias source is introduced. The experiment therefore provided a clear understanding of waveform shaping using diodes and its usefulness in signal conditioning and protection circuits.
