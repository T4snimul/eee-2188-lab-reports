\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

The experiment confirmed that diode clippers remove the selected half-cycle of an input waveform while clampers shift the output level without removing the waveform shape, demonstrating the expected behavior of both signal-limiting and level-shifting circuits. Overall, the results provide a clear understanding of how diode-based waveform shaping circuits can be used in practical signal-conditioning and voltage-level adjustment applications.
