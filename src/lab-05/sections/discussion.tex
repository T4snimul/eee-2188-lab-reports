\section{Discussion}
\markright{Discussion}

The experimental results showed that the clipper circuits behaved as expected, with the diode allowing current only in one direction and thereby removing one portion of the input waveform. The unbiased positive and negative clippers produced the expected output shapes, while the biased circuits shifted the clipping level according to the applied DC voltage. Minor deviations in the observed waveforms may be attributed to the practical effects of diode forward voltage drop, component tolerances, and measurement limitations, but the overall behavior remained consistent with the theory described in \cite{lab_05_boylstad}.
