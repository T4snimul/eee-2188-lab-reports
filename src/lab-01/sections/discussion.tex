\section{Discussion}
\markright{Discussion}

During this experiment, we observed that while multimeters are sufficient for measuring static DC values, the oscilloscope is essential for visualizing how signals (like those from the function generator) change over time. We also noted the critical importance of component polarity; connecting electrolytic capacitors or LEDs in reverse can lead to circuit failure or component damage. Furthermore, the distinction between active components (which require a power supply to function, like Op-Amps) and passive components (like resistors) was verified through their physical construction and datasheet specifications.
