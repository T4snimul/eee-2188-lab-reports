\subsection{Function Generator}
\markright{Function Generator}

\subsubsection*{Definition}
A function generator is an electronic instrument that produces periodic waveforms of
controlled frequency, amplitude, and shape \cite{horowitz2015}. Common output waveforms
include sine, square, triangle, sawtooth, and arbitrary user-defined shapes. The
instrument in this laboratory is the \textbf{Protek 9205C Sweep Function Generator},
which also supports frequency sweep (varying the output frequency over a defined range),
useful for testing frequency-dependent circuits such as filters and amplifiers.

\subsubsection*{Specifications}
The Protek 9205C covers output frequencies from below 1\,Hz up to approximately
5\,MHz across its sine, square, and triangle outputs, with output amplitude adjustable
from millivolts to several volts peak-to-peak into 50\,$\Omega$ loads. Frequency
accuracy is governed by a crystal-controlled timebase.

\subsubsection*{Purpose of Use}
Function generators supply the test signals needed to characterize amplifier gain and
bandwidth, filter frequency response, oscillator start-up behavior, and digital logic
timing margins. The sweep function automates frequency-response measurements.

\subsubsection*{Applications}
Function generators are used in amplifier characterization, filter testing, transducer
excitation, lock-in amplifier reference supply, and educational circuit experiments
across all frequency ranges \cite{horowitz2015}.
