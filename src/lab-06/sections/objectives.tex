\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
        \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
  \item To study the forward and reverse bias characteristics of a Zener diode.
  \item To determine the Zener voltage $V_Z$ and observe the breakdown region.
  \item To measure the current $I_Z$ through the diode for different applied voltages.
  \item To plot the $V_Z$-$I_Z$ characteristic curve and verify the voltage regulation property of the Zener diode.
\end{enumerate}
