\section{Simulation}
\markright{Simulation}

The full-wave bridge rectifier circuit was simulated using LTspice to verify the theoretical predictions. Figure \ref{fig:lab04_ltspice_schematic} shows the simulated circuit with a center-tapped transformer (modeled with coupled inductors), four 1N4007 diodes, a 10\,k$\Omega$ load resistor, and a 10\,$\mu$F filter capacitor.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.75\textwidth]{lab-04/assets/center-tapped-without-capacitor-schematic.png}
  \caption{LTspice simulation schematic of the center-tapped full-wave rectifier circuit showing the input voltage source, transformer, four diodes, load resistor, and measurement nodes.}
  \label{fig:lab04_ltspice_schematic}
\end{figure}

The simulation results confirm that the full-wave rectifier effectively converts both positive and negative half-cycles of the AC input into DC output pulses. The output voltage demonstrates characteristic double-frequency ripple at twice the input frequency. When the filter capacitor is included, the ripple is significantly reduced, and the output voltage becomes nearly constant.
