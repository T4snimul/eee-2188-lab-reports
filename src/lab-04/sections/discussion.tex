\section{Discussion}
\markright{Discussion}

During the experiment we encountered problems with the oscilloscope, intermittent triggering, noisy traces, and apparent probe/grounding issues, which produced distorted and inconsistent waveform readings. We also tried to simulate the center-tapped transformer and it worked as expected. These instrument problems led to unreliable peak and RMS measurements and increased apparent ripple, causing our numerical results (loss, efficiency and ripple) to deviate significantly from theoretical expectations. Because the scope behavior affected multiple runs, the trends observed (for example, that adding a capacitor reduces ripple) remain qualitatively valid, but the quantitative values should be regarded as tentative until measurements are repeated with corrected probe compensation, secure ground connections, and verified timebase/trigger settings.
