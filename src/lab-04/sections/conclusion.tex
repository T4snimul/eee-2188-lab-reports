\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

The experiment demonstrates the expected qualitative behavior of rectifier circuits and filter capacitors: adding a capacitor reduced output ripple and improved DC stability. The observed trends are consistent with theoretical expectations and support the usefulness of filter capacitors in smoothing rectified outputs. Some variation in the measured values was also observed during the experiment, but the overall results still confirm that the rectifier and filter combination performs as intended.
