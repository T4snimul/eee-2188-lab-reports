\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item To study the construction and operation of a p-n junction diode.
    \item To understand the behavior of a silicon p-n junction diode under forward and reverse bias.
    \item To obtain the forward and reverse V--I characteristics of a silicon p-n junction diode.
    \item To identify the cut-in voltage, reverse saturation behavior, and practical diode applications.
\end{enumerate}
