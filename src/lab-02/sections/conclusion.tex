\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

The V--I characteristics of a silicon p-n junction diode were studied in forward and
reverse bias and matched the expected nonlinear behavior. In forward bias, the current
increased with applied voltage, while in reverse bias the current was so small that our
multimeter did not show it with the $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ resistor used in the setup. A
$1\,\text{k}\Omega$ resistor would likely have allowed some reverse current to be
observed more clearly. Overall, the experiment verified the diode's cut-in behavior,
reverse blocking action, and usefulness as a unidirectional device.
