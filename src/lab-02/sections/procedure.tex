\section{Experimental Procedure}
\markright{Experimental Procedure}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item The circuit was assembled in forward bias according to the circuit diagram, with the 1N4007
        diode connected in series with the $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ resistor and the digital multimeter used to
        measure the diode current and voltage.

  \item The DC supply voltage was increased gradually in small steps, and the corresponding forward
        voltage across the diode and forward current were recorded in Table~\ref
        {tab:lab2_forward_observation}.

  \item A forward V--I graph was prepared using the applied diode voltage on the X-axis and the
        current on the Y-axis.

  \item The connections were then reversed for reverse bias by connecting the diode in the opposite
        polarity while keeping the same series resistor and measurement arrangement.

  \item The reverse supply voltage was increased gradually within the safe operating range of the
        diode, and the corresponding reverse voltage and current were recorded in Table~\ref
        {tab:lab2_reverse_observation}.

  \item A reverse V--I graph was prepared using the applied reverse diode voltage on the X-axis and
        the current on the Y-axis.
\end{enumerate}
