\section{Experimental Procedure}
\markright{Experimental Procedure}

The experimental steps were followed according to the supplied laboratory manual for
Experiment No. 02 \cite{lab2_manual}.

\subsection*{Forward Bias}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Connections were made according to the forward-bias circuit diagram.
    \item The positive terminal of the DC supply was connected to the anode of the diode through the $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ resistor.
    \item The negative terminal of the supply was connected to the cathode of the diode.
    \item The supply voltage was increased gradually in small steps.
    \item For each step, the voltage across the diode and the corresponding forward current were recorded.
    \item A graph was plotted by taking diode voltage $V_F$ along the X-axis and forward current $I_F$ along the Y-axis.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Reverse Bias}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Connections were made according to the reverse-bias circuit diagram.
    \item The negative terminal of the DC supply was connected to the anode of the diode.
    \item The positive terminal of the supply was connected to the cathode of the diode through the $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ resistor.
    \item The supply voltage was increased gradually while keeping the voltage safely below the breakdown rating of the diode.
    \item The reverse voltage and reverse current were recorded for each applied voltage.
    \item A graph was plotted by taking reverse diode voltage $V_R$ along the X-axis and reverse current $I_R$ along the Y-axis.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Precautions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Proper meter ranges were selected before taking measurements.
    \item Diode polarity was checked carefully before applying the supply voltage.
    \item All breadboard connections were made tight and verified before switching on the power supply.
    \item The reverse bias voltage was kept within the safe operating range of the diode.
\end{enumerate}
