\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

This experiment helped in understanding the basic concepts of Object-Oriented Programming in C++. By working with classes and objects, it became clear how data and functions can be combined into a single unit to model real-world problems. The use of constructors, copy constructors, and destructors showed how objects are initialized, copied, and removed during program execution. Overall, the experiment demonstrated that OOP makes programs more organized, reusable, and easier to manage compared with simple procedural programming.
