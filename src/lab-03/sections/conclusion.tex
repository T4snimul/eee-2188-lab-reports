\section{Conclusion}
\markright{Conclusion}

This experiment demonstrated the basic operation of a half-wave rectifier and confirmed that a single diode can convert AC into pulsating DC for different input waveforms. The results showed that the square wave produced the highest efficiency and the sinusoidal wave the lowest, mainly because the waveform shape affects the RMS value and the amount of residual AC after rectification. The high ripple factors also showed that a simple half-wave circuit is not suitable for producing a smooth DC output, which supports the need for full-wave rectification or filtering in practical power supply designs.
